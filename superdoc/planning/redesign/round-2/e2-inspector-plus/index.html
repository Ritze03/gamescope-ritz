E2 · Inspector Rail, deepened — Depth on Demand

Direction E's three regions, pushed as far as the Inspector can carry. Everything that explains, derives, qualifies, tunes or measures has left the Sheet. What remains is the irreducible minimum: what a setting is called, what it is set to, and how to change it — one row height, one label line, one right-bound control line, top to bottom. The Inspector has two modes — CONFIGURE (what this does, and the values you can set) and DETAILS (everything technical) — and a structural law that makes it incapable of becoming a junk drawer: it has no authoring API at all. Nothing is written into it; it is rendered by the shell from the selected row's registration.
The controls are direction B's, uplifted ~25% the way shipping issue #23 uplifted the handoff baseline, and snapped to one control height — --H = 28 base units, which every control's hit box honours.
Everything below is live. Click a rail item, click a row, drag a slider, flip a switch, type in a text field, filter the log, drag the display_scale and accent hue sliders, and press Ctrl+K.

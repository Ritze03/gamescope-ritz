One fixed slab replaces the dock and the six floating windows: a Rail of categories, a Sheet of settings, and an Inspector that always describes the selected row. Every tab bar in the product is gone — Display's four tabs became rail items, and System Monitor's six became a list plus the inspector.
Start on 1 · Default to see the calm case, then 2 · 2.0× collapsed — the responsive ladder gives the sheet more width at 2.0× than at 1.25×, because collapsing the rail and floating the inspector returns more space than the scale-up costs. 7 · Control taxonomy shows every permitted control once; it is the rule set that makes an inconsistent screen hard to build. Each screen carries a caption at the bottom-left explaining what to look at.
